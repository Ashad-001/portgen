Guidance

What makes a great portfolio submission

Follow these pointers while you fill the form so your midterm submission feels clear and complete.

Be concise

Keep your bio under 3-4 sentences. Lead with your role, what you build, and the value you create.

List strengths

  • Pick 3-6 skills that reflect your strongest areas.
  • Use commas to separate skills (e.g., HTML, CSS, JavaScript).

Match your audience

Highlight coursework and projects that align with Web Technologies 2 outcomes—clean code and responsive design.

Preview checklist
  • Full name appears correctly
  • Title matches your focus
  • Bio is short and clear
  • Skills are comma-separated
Ready for class
